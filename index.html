FAQ

Got questions?

What is oko?

oko is a free BDIX browser for Android — browse and stream content from BDIX FTP servers in Bangladesh, with a clean, fast interface on mobile and Android TV.

Does oko require an internet connection?

oko works through local BDIX connections. You need to be connected to a supported BDIX network to access content.

Is oko free to use?

Yes, oko is free to use. It is supported by ads served through Google AdMob. Get it on Google Play.

Does oko collect any personal data?

oko requires no account. Google AdMob may collect device information and ad identifiers to serve ads. Watch history, settings and downloads stay on your device. See the privacy policy for details.

What are the minimum device requirements?

oko runs on Android 5.0 (Lollipop) and above, including Android TV. It is lightweight by design and performs well across a wide range of hardware.
