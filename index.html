FAQ

Got questions?

What is oko?

oko is a lightweight client for accessing and streaming content from your local BDIX FTP servers. It delivers a clean, fast interface without the overhead of a full browser.

Does oko require an internet connection?

oko works through local BDIX connections. You need to be connected to a supported BDIX network to access content.

Is oko free to use?

Yes, oko is completely free to use with no hidden charges or subscriptions. You can download the latest version directly using the download button above.

Does oko collect any personal data?

oko does not require an account and does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. We use anonymous usage analytics to understand how the app is used and to improve the experience over time. No personally identifiable data is ever involved.

What are the minimum device requirements?

oko is compatible with Android 5.0 (Lollipop) and above for mobile devices, and Android 8 (Oreo) and above for Android TV. It is lightweight by design and performs well across a wide range of hardware.
