PRIVACY POLICY

Effective Date: July 12, 2026

oko ("the Application") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

1. Data We Collect

  • Device information (model, operating system, screen size)
  • Advertising identifier
  • Approximate location (derived from IP address)
  • App usage data (features used, session duration)
  • Crash and error reports
  • Network connection type

2. How We Use This Data

  • Google AdMob uses device information and advertising identifier to serve relevant advertisements. Ad interactions (impressions and clicks) are measured for ad performance.
  • Firebase Analytics uses app usage data to help us understand how the Application is used and improve it. Crash and error reports help us fix bugs and improve stability.

All data collected by these services is used to serve ads and is not directly tied to your name or contact details.

3. Local Data Storage

The following information is stored only on your device and is never sent to us or any external server:

  • Watch history
  • Theme preferences
  • Downloaded media files
  • App settings

4. Children's Privacy

The Application is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

5. Your Choices

  • You can opt out of personalized ads through your device settings
  • You can delete all local data by uninstalling the Application
  • For data collected by Google services, you can manage your preferences through Google's privacy settings

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the Application's update notes. Continued use constitutes acceptance.

7. Contact

For privacy-related inquiries, contact us at officialoko@proton.me.

8. Governing Law

This Privacy Policy is governed by the laws of Bangladesh.

By using oko, you confirm that you have read, understood, and agreed to this privacy policy.
